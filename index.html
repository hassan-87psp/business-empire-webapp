Privacy by design

Your business information stays focused on your workspace.

Business Empire is built around the records you choose to enter. The app does not use third-party advertising or tracking and does not sell personal information for targeted advertising.

Business Empire uses Google Firebase services for authentication, storage, notifications and crash reporting. See the Privacy Policy for complete details.

✓No third-party advertising or advertising profiles.
✓No collection of precise or approximate device location.
✓Workspace owners control which businesses members can access.
✓Account deletion requests can be made by email and are completed within 30 days after confirmation.
✓Supporting receipts and files are accessed only when you choose to attach them.
